These exercises vary the observation scheme or sampling rule. For conditional probabilities, write the likelihood of the complete record and check whether missingness or selection changes the law. Conditional expectation is the optimal squared-loss predictor when the target has a finite second moment.

Classify pairs of counted objects by their overlap when computing variances. For stopped costs, ask whether the event that a draw occurs depends only on earlier observations, then use independence of the fresh increment from the past. In distribution calculations, distinguish uniform selection of a spacing from selection proportional to its length, and check endpoints, normalization, and scaling. Marginal sign probabilities alone cannot determine independence of joint sign events.
